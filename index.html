"वायुः पित्तं कफश्चेति त्रयो दोषाः समासतः।
विकृताविकृता देहं घ्नन्ति ते वर्तयन्ति च ॥"

— Ashtanga Hridayam, Sutrasthana (1.6)

English Translation: "Vata, Pitta, and Kapha are the three operational life forces (Doshas) governing the human ecosystem. When they exist in their pristine, balanced parameters, they sustain and nourish life. The exact millisecond they fall out of balance, they introduce decay and disease."

The Biological Law: According to Ayurveda, an imbalance in one or more of these life forces is the fundamental cause of both physical and mental illness. The word dosha translates to 'fault' and the reason why the life forces are called faults is not because there is something wrong in the life force itself, but because they have an innate tendency to move towards disease, if balance is not maintained. This is the reason why Vagabhata, the author of Ashtanga Hridayam, which translates to the heart or essence of the eight limbs, says that an imbalance leads to decay and disease. The forces that are responsible for vitality are also responsible for destruction if they are excessively imbalanced.
